# Q&A for the Kubernetes workflows.

= k8s-first-look
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Before any kubectl work on a new or unfamiliar cluster: confirm which cluster and namespace you are pointed at.
q: What is the most common costly mistake?
a: Running a change against the wrong context. Read kubectl config current-context first, and practise on a local kind cluster.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 2 runs your first pod.

= k8s-pod-hello
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Trying an image quickly, or learning how a pod starts and what its events say.
q: Why not run bare pods in production?
a: Nothing recreates a deleted or crashed bare pod. Use a Deployment.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 3 adds self-healing and updates.

= k8s-deployment-rollout
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Running a stateless application with several copies that must stay up during updates.
q: What happens when a rollout goes wrong?
a: New pods fail to become ready while the old ones keep serving. kubectl rollout undo returns to the last good version.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 4 gives the pods a stable address.

= k8s-service-expose
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Other pods or users need a stable name and address for a changing set of pods.
q: What does an empty ENDPOINTS list mean?
a: The selector matches no ready pod. Compare the service selector with the pod labels and check readiness.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 5 moves settings out of the image.

= k8s-config-secrets
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: The same image must run in dev and prod with different settings, or needs a password.
q: Is a Secret encrypted?
a: Not by default. It is base64 encoded. Limit access with RBAC and enable encryption at rest or use an external secret store.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 6 keeps data beyond a pod's life.

= k8s-storage
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: A database, upload folder or any data that must survive pod restarts.
q: Deployment or StatefulSet for a database?
a: StatefulSet. It gives stable names and a separate disk per pod.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 7 brings outside traffic in.

= k8s-ingress-dns
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Several web services share one public address and need host or path routing and HTTPS.
q: Why is my Ingress ignored?
a: There is no ingress controller, or ingressClassName does not match the controller's class.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 8 makes pods healthy and well sized.

= k8s-probes-resources
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Pods receive traffic before they are ready, hang without restarting, or hog a node.
q: Readiness or liveness?
a: Readiness controls traffic. Liveness restarts a stuck container. A wrong liveness probe can cause restart loops.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 9 scales on load.

= k8s-scaling-hpa
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Traffic changes over the day and you want pods added and removed automatically.
q: Why does the HPA show unknown?
a: Metrics-server is missing, or the pods have no CPU request to compare with.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 10 controls who can do what.

= k8s-rbac
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Giving a person, pipeline or pod only the access it needs.
q: How do I check what an account can do?
a: kubectl auth can-i with --as, or --list for everything.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 11 packages and reuses manifests.

= k8s-helm-kustomize
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: The same application must be deployed many times with different values, or you install third-party software.
q: Helm or Kustomize?
a: Helm for packaged, versioned applications. Kustomize for patching your own plain YAML per environment.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 12 fixes what breaks.

= k8s-debug
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: A pod is Pending, crashing, not pulling its image, or a service does not answer.
q: What is the first command?
a: kubectl describe on the object, and read the Events at the bottom.
q: What comes next?
a: Module 13 covers planned node work.

= k8s-node-maintenance
q: In which situation do we use this?
a: Patching, resizing or replacing a node, or upgrading the cluster.
q: What must I always do after a drain?
a: Uncordon the node and confirm no node shows SchedulingDisabled.
q: What comes next?
a: Combine these skills with the Terraform and Jenkins topics for full delivery.
